03 / Labor context · use with care

A useful signal,
with a clear boundary.

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reports that employment for Project Management Specialists is projected to grow 7% from 2025 to 2035, with about 76,500 annual openings on average. The May 2025 median annual wage for that occupation is $102,320.

These are BLS figures for Project Management Specialists (SOC 13-1082), not Product Managers. They are adjacent context about coordinating work; they are not a Product Manager job outlook or salary estimate.
